THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

About Me

A researcher’s curiosity, a designer’s mindset and a computer scientist’s perspective.

Understanding people. Designing better experiences.

What motivates me most about technology is its potential to improve people's everyday lives. From a young age, I often helped family members with technical issues, which gradually made me realize the importance of usability and user-friendly design. While I only fully recognized this motivation during my studies, the desire to help others through technology has long been a part of what drives me.

After graduating from high school, I was unsure what I wanted to study. My interest in technology eventually led me to pursue a degree in computer science. However, as my studies progressed, I discovered that my interests lay less in programming and more in understanding how people interact with technology, what challenges they face and how thoughtful design can help overcome those challenges. This realization led me towards the field of Human–Technology Interaction.

My studies in Human–Technology Interaction at Tampere University have allowed me to turn this interest into practical expertise in user research, usability evaluation and human-centered design. Through collaboration with industry partners, I have learned to translate research findings into meaningful design decisions that address real user needs.

I enjoy investigating problems, understanding different perspectives and exploring how digital experiences can be improved. I am particularly passionate about accessibility and ethical design, as I believe technology should empower people rather than create unnecessary barriers.

Ultimately, I want my work to make a positive difference in people's lives by helping them interact with technology effortlessly, confidently and independently.
