Making a local business website easier to use

  • What I did Evaluated the design with usability heuristics and WCAG, checked the structure and navigation, and worked closely with the developer on changes.

Project Description

Trendy-kirppis needed a new website for its business. I joined the client project as a usability and accessibility specialist, working alongside the developer rather than building the site myself.

Problem Statement

Visitors needed to find relevant information and navigate the new site efficiently. The challenge was to evaluate the developing interface against usability and accessibility principles and identify changes while they were still practical to implement.

My Contribution

I provided feedback on design decisions and assessed information architecture and navigation. My role was to challenge and refine the design through evaluation, while the developer was responsible for implementation.

Research & Evaluation

I carried out an expert evaluation of the website. I used usability heuristics to find usability problems and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) to check that people with different needs could use the site. I also reviewed the information architecture and the navigation paths to see whether visitors could reach content in a logical and efficient way.

Solution & Recommendations

I communicated findings as concrete change suggestions, discussing alternatives directly with the developer so the feedback could inform implementation.

Outcomes & Learnings

The client received actionable feedback on usability, accessibility and navigation. The collaboration taught me how to translate evaluation findings into practical recommendations for a developer. No formal post-change user testing results are documented here.
