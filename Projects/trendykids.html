Project Description

Trendy-kirppis is a children’s flea market company that needed a new website. A developer built the site, and I worked closely with them as the usability and accessibility specialist. I evaluated the design using usability heuristics and WCAG, checked that the information architecture and navigation paths were suitable and efficient, and gave feedback on design choices and suggestions for changes.

Problem Statement

The client needed a new website for their company. The site was being built by a developer, and before it could serve customers well, it needed to be checked for usability and accessibility, and to have a clear structure and navigation that let visitors find what they need efficiently.

My Contribution

I did not design or build the website. I worked as the usability and accessibility specialist and collaborated closely with the developer throughout. I gave feedback on the design choices, suggested changes, and made sure the information architecture and navigation paths were suitable and efficient.

Research & Evaluation

I carried out an expert evaluation of the website. I used usability heuristics to find usability problems and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) to check that people with different needs could use the site. I also reviewed the information architecture and the navigation paths to see whether visitors could reach content in a logical and efficient way.

Solution & Recommendations

I passed my findings to the developer as feedback on specific design choices, together with suggestions for how to change them. Because we worked closely together, the developer could ask questions and discuss alternatives while making the improvements.

Outcomes & Learnings

The developer received concrete feedback and change suggestions based on the evaluation, and the site’s structure and navigation paths were checked to be suitable and efficient. The project taught me to communicate evaluation findings in a way that is clear and practical for the person building the product, and gave me experience of working with a real client and developer.
